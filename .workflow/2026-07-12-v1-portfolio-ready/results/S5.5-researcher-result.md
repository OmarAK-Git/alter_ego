## Acceptance criteria (from packet)

1. Staleness halt preserved: age > `max_profile_staleness_days` → `staleness_halt`, score 0.
2. Halt + uncleared alert (`new|acknowledged|investigating`) → `staleness_active_alert_escalation` (+ `sensor_health_staleness` OK).
3. Halt without active alert → escalation flag absent.
4. `GET /api/mandatory-escalations` lists items without requiring `is_anomaly=True`.
5. Queue items link to uncleared alert `decision_id`(s) for entity.
6. Clear via existing workflow removes from mandatory queue.
7. Extend-halt with required justification persisted (schema expansion allowed).
8. Pytest covers scorer combo + API queue.

## Design notes

- Active alert = `is_anomaly=True` decision for entity with workflow state in `{new, acknowledged, investigating}` (default `new` when no row).
- Mandatory queue is query-time: entity has escalation-flagged halt decision **and** active uncleared alert(s), unless a non-expired extend-halt exists.
- SLA hours may be hardcoded (config weights/thresholds out of scope).
